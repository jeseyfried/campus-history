# Campus History Brand Kit

## Direction

Campus History should feel like a classic cultural magazine applied to a public university archive: serious, readable, image-led, and a little sharp. The model is Smithsonian-style editorial confidence: large serif headlines, generous story rhythm, strong rules, tight labels, and archival photographs treated as primary evidence. UNM enters through color, campus texture, and restrained Southwestern accents.

## Palette

Use the official UNM palette as accents, not as a busy rainbow.

| Role | Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `--ink` | `#171412` | Primary text, rules, editorial borders |
| Paper | `--paper` | `#fbf8f1` | Page background |
| Cherry | `--unm-cherry` | `#ba0c2f` | Primary accent, labels, hero edge stripe |
| Turquoise | `--unm-turquoise` | `#007a86` | Secondary accent, hover, section bands |
| High Noon | `--unm-high-noon` | `#ffc600` | Small color pop under headings and labels |
| Terra | `--unm-terra` | `#c05131` | Warm alternate accent |
| Mesa | `--unm-mesa` | `#d6a461` | Quiet archival warmth |
| Lobo Gray | `--unm-lobo-gray` | `#63666a` | Muted metadata and captions |

Avoid using more than two bright accents in one component. The default pairing is Cherry plus High Noon; use Turquoise for links, section structure, and interactive states.

## Typography

Display: `Libre Baskerville`, used for big story titles and major headings.

Body: `Source Serif 4`, used for essay paragraphs and longform reading.

Interface: `Source Sans 3`, used for navigation, captions, metadata, cards, labels, and controls.

Mono: `Fira Mono`, used for captions where an archival/documentary tone helps.

Body text should be comfortably readable, around 18px, with open line-height. Do not shrink longform essays to dashboard scale.

## Editorial Elements

Use heavy black rules and small uppercase labels to give pages magazine structure. Section headings should feel like article breaks, not generic webpage headings.

Figures should have visible borders and captions that read as documentary evidence. Pullquotes should be large, serif, and bordered with Cherry; use them to make an essay sparkle without turning every paragraph into decoration.

Cards should look like article teasers: photo first, label, strong headline, short description. Keep cards square-edged and restrained.

## Tone

Classic, archival, precise, and alive. The site should not feel like a marketing landing page, a beige academic handout, or a maximalist UNM homepage. Let color appear as punctuation.
